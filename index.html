    Import trades

    Live price connection

    Live prices come from Finnhub. Create a free account, copy your API key and paste it below. Without a key the dashboard shows simulated demo prices.

    Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage).

    Price history & gold/silver

    Stock growth charts and gold/silver prices come from Twelve Data (free: 800 requests a day). Without a key the charts use simulated demo data, or the prices recorded while you have the app open.

    How live updates work

    • The app runs in your browser. While the page is open, stock prices stream live from Finnhub during market hours and refresh every minute. Gold and silver refresh every 10 minutes.
    • Nothing runs while the page is closed. When you open it again it fetches the latest prices straight away.
    • No server or Claude connector is needed, only the two free API keys above.

    Your data

    Holdings and income are saved in this browser. Export a backup to move them to another device.

    How screening works

    Each stock is checked against two screens, following AAOIFI-style standards:

    1. Business screen - the core business must not be riba (interest-based finance), alcohol, music & entertainment, gambling, tobacco, pork, or conventional insurance.
    2. Financial screen - impermissible revenue under 5%, interest-bearing debt and cash each under ~33% of market cap.

    Compliant stocks with a small impermissible share require purification: give that percentage of dividends to charity.

    Classifications here are estimates for guidance only, not a fatwa. Confirm with a dedicated screener (Zoya, Musaffa, IdealRatings) or a qualified scholar. You can override any stock's status from its details panel.
